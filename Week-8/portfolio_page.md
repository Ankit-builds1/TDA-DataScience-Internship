# Portfolio Page: Customer Churn Reduction Capstone

## Business Question

How can a subscription-style business reduce customer churn and protect recurring revenue?

## Methods

- Data cleaning and quality checks
- Exploratory data analysis
- Customer segmentation
- Hypothesis testing
- Logistic regression
- Business implementation planning

## Outcome

The analysis found a churn rate of 10.6%. Contract type, payment method, tenure, and monthly charges were used to prioritize customers for retention outreach.

## Recommendation

Launch a four-week retention sprint focused on high-risk, high-value customers and measure saved monthly recurring revenue.
